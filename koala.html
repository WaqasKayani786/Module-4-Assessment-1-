ANIMALS MAP PLACES

KOALA

Koalas are small marsupials native to Australia. They are famous for their round faces, fluffy ears and grey fur.

Koalas spend most of their lives in eucalyptus trees. They mainly eat eucalyptus leaves and can sleep for many hours each day.

Koalas have strong claws that help them climb trees. They are mostly active at night and spend much of their time resting during the day.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND THEM

The koalas are in the lower right of the park, at the edge of the giraffe enclosure.
