ANIMALS MAP PLACES

MONKEY TRAIL

Follow the Monkey Trail and watch the playful monkeys as they climb, jump and explore their surroundings.

WHAT WILL YOU SEE?

  • Different species of monkeys
  • Monkeys climbing and playing
  • Interesting facts about monkey behaviour

WHERE WILL YOU FIND IT?

The Monkey Trail is on the left side of the park, above the main entrance building. Follow the blue stream past the flamingo pond to find the monkeys.
