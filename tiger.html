TIGER

Tigers are the largest members of the cat family. They are easily recognised by their orange coats, black stripes and powerful bodies.

Tigers are carnivores and hunt animals such as deer and wild pigs. They are excellent swimmers and often spend time near rivers, lakes and other sources of water.

Every tiger has a unique pattern of stripes. Tigers are generally solitary animals and usually hunt alone.
