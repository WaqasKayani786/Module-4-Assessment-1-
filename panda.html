ANIMALS MAP PLACES

PANDA

Giant pandas are mammals native to the mountain forests of central China. They are famous for their distinctive black and white fur.

Pandas mainly eat bamboo, even though they belong to the order of carnivorous mammals. They spend many hours each day eating because bamboo contains relatively little energy.

Pandas are excellent climbers and can also swim. They usually live alone and spend much of their time searching for food.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND THEM

The pandas are in Panda Canyon at the top of the park, right under the cable car line.
