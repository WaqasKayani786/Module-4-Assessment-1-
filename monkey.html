ANIMALS MAP PLACES

MONKEY

Monkeys are intelligent and active primates that live in many different environments around the world. They come in many different shapes and sizes.

Depending on the species, monkeys may eat fruit, leaves, seeds, insects and other foods. Many monkeys use their hands and tails to help them climb.

Monkeys are highly social animals and often live in groups. They communicate with each other using sounds, movements and facial expressions.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND THEM

The monkeys are on the Monkey Trail on the left side of the park, above the main entrance building. Follow the blue stream past the flamingo pond to find them.
