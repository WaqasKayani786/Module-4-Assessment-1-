ANIMALS MAP PLACES

LION

Lions are large cats that live mainly in Africa. They are easily recognised by their powerful bodies and, in males, their impressive manes.

Lions are carnivores and hunt animals such as antelope, zebra and buffalo. Unlike most cats, lions live and hunt in social groups called prides.

Lions spend much of their day resting and become more active during cooler parts of the day. They use teamwork when hunting larger prey.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND THEM

The lions are on the right side of the park, just below Northern Frontier and above the elephants.
