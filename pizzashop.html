PIZZA SHOP

Feeling hungry after exploring the park? Stop by the pizza shop for a delicious and filling meal.

WHAT CAN YOU ENJOY?

  • Freshly made pizzas
  • A variety of tasty toppings

WHERE WILL YOU FIND IT?

The pizza shop is in the food area at the top left of the park, next to the wooden boardwalk and the cable car line.
